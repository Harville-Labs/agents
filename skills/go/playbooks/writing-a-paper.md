### Writing a paper

**You own the claim and every citation. Delegate the reading and the reviews.** For turning an experiment's result into a paper or preprint. A doc, README, or blog post is not a paper. Use the **technical-writing** skill for those.

1. Run the **write-paper** skill. Copy its numbered stages into the todo list.
2. Treat stage 1 as a gate. When the result does not hold up, report that and stop. Don't draft around it.
3. Build `paper/paper.pdf` after each stage from figures on, and look at every page before you move on.
4. Never submit the paper anywhere. The human authors submit it.
